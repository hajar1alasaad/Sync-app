📱 **Sync** is a mobile app that helps you step out and meet people around you in the real world right now, instead of just chatting behind screens. 💬❌

### 🗺️ Radar & Live Map:
📍 When you open the app, it shows a map of your area.

👀 You can see nearby activities (within a 5 km radius) and people who want to hang out or do something at that exact moment. 🎯

### 🚀 Create a Gathering (`Launch Now`):
➕ If you want to go out, you tap this button and choose what you want to do (grab coffee ☕, play sports ⚽, try a new restaurant 🍕).

📅 Set the time and venue, and your broadcast appears on the map for nearby people to join. 📡

### ⭐ Reliability Score & Trust:
🛡️ To prevent people from flaking, every profile has a reliability percentage (e.g., 100%). 💯

✅ If a user shows up on time, their score stays high. 👍

❌ If they ghost or cancel last minute, their rating drops. 📉

🤝 This ensures you only meet dependable people. 🔒

### 🏅 Digital Attendance Badges (NFTs):
🎖️ These are simple "digital medals" added to your profile whenever you physically attend an event. 🎟️

Verified proof that you are an active community member. 🌐

### 💳 Wallet & Payments:
💰 If an activity requires splitting a bill or booking a venue (like renting a football pitch), you can securely deposit or pay money directly inside the app. 🔐

⚡ Using crypto or electronic payments effortlessly. 🪙

### 💬 Group Chat & Moments:
👥 A temporary group chat automatically opens for participants joining the same meetup so you can coordinate details. 📲

📸 Alongside a story feature (Moments) to share quick photos and videos from the event. 🎬

---

### 🌟 Core Goal of the App:
✨ To turn mindless scrolling into real-world hangouts with like-minded people nearby, instantly and safely. 🤝
